A real open language model scored by the same lexical grader as the deterministic stubs, over the same questions. The stubs are kept in the run because a model's accuracy means nothing without the behaviours the grader was built to separate sitting beside it. Replies reach the grader verbatim: nothing repairs, reformats or re-asks. A failed call is recorded as a failed call and scored as the empty answer it was, rather than being replaced by a refusal the model never gave.
